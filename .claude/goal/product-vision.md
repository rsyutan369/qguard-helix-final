# QGuard Helix Product Vision

Version: 2.0
Status: Active
Audience: Product Owners, Architects, Engineers, AI Agents

# Vision

QGuard Helix is an Enterprise Post-Quantum Cryptography (PQC) Migration, Discovery, Telemetry, Governance, Trust, and Crypto-Agility Platform that enables organizations to understand, modernize, govern, and continuously improve their cryptographic ecosystem throughout the transition to the post-quantum era.

The platform serves as the enterprise operating platform for cryptographic modernization by providing complete visibility into cryptographic assets, assessing quantum readiness, orchestrating migration, enforcing governance, delivering runtime intelligence, and enabling continuous crypto agility.

---

# Mission

Enable organizations to confidently transition from classical cryptography to post-quantum cryptography through discovery, assessment, migration, governance, observability, intelligence, and continuous trust.

QGuard Helix empowers security teams to make informed decisions using accurate data, real-time telemetry, AI-assisted insights, and enterprise-grade governance.
