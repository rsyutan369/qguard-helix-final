## Claim guardrails (reminder)
- ✅ "post-quantum," "end-to-end encrypted," "zero-knowledge," "NIST FIPS 203/204," "forward secrecy," "not even QGuard can read…"
- 🚫 "unhackable," "unbreakable," "100% secure," "impossible to intercept." MITM protection = the user verifying the safety number, so phrase it as "verify and no one in the middle can read it."
