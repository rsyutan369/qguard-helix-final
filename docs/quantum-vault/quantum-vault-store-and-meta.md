## Claim guardrails (reminder)
- ✅ "post-quantum," "strictly on-device / 100% local," "zero-knowledge," "NIST FIPS 203/204 (Level 5)," "tamper-evident," "any file size," "quantum-safe recovery."
- 🚫 "unhackable," "unbreakable," "100% secure." ML-KEM protects *keys*, AES-256-GCM encrypts *files*. Always pair the recovery story with "back up your recovery key — lose both it and your device and the files are unrecoverable."
